# CPDSE gateway content: impact and responsibilities

Working draft for improving `CPDSE_gateway_updated_circles_fundamentals.xlsx`.

Each circle, service and Fundamental area has:

- an **impact statement**: the high-level purpose, written as *"We … so that … can …"*;
- **responsibilities**: what it answers for on an ongoing basis. These are not tasks
  to check off; a responsibility stays true for as long as the circle exists.

IDs match the spreadsheet so the text can be copied back. Sources: the WoW 2.0 pages
in `cpdse-okf-vault` (Season 4, Sept 2026) and the circle and service projects in
Basecamp. Anything inferred rather than stated in a source is marked *(inferred)*.

---

## Services (`CIR_SERVICES`)

**Impact:** We turn CPDSE's data-science and AI expertise into services for the
pharma schools, so that educators, researchers and research groups can keep pace with
a field that AI is changing faster than universities usually can.

**Responsibilities**
- Keep an overview of demand for CPDSE services and of the people available to meet it.
- Make sure each service has a lead and delivers what it promises.
- Match incoming requests to the right service and the right people.
- Protect capacity: only take on work the centre can deliver well.
- Professionalise how services are offered, delivered and followed up.
- Draw on the Research Community for research-based content, so that staff orchestrate
  and researchers and students deliver.
- Report on service activity and impact to the Nucleus and the Mission circle.

Portfolio lead: Lykke Pedersen. Meets every third week (portfolio sync).
Basecamp: none found. The WoW 2.0 announcement mentions a "Circle:Services" project,
but it is not among the projects you can access; each service has its own project.

### Curriculum Upgrade (`SV01`)

*The spreadsheet calls this "Curriculum Development"; the vault, Basecamp and the
WoW 2.0 announcement call it "Curriculum Upgrade".*

**Impact:** We work with teachers at SDU and UCPH to build data science and AI into the
pharma curricula, so that graduates leave with the skills that modern pharmaceutical
work requires.

**Responsibilities**
- Keep an overview of which courses include data-science elements, and how far
  students progress through them across a programme, mapped to the CPDSE competency model.
- Work with course teams to agree and prioritise which courses to upgrade.
- Support teachers in designing and introducing data-science and AI elements in
  their courses.
- Make sure the teaching infrastructure (servers, apps, Git) supports what the
  courses need.
- Bring in advice from the Curriculum Transformation Board and from industry on what
  curricula need to adapt to.
- Follow up on the effect of upgrades, for example through learning analytics and
  educational research.
- Keep the public overview of CPDSE's educational offer up to date.

**Wanna help?** Help shape what the next generation of pharmacists learns about data
science and AI. Talk to Lykke (UCPH) or Sebastian (SDU). Open asks right now:
- Join the systematic review of educational research literature: synthesis, write-up and presentation.
- Help design proper course evaluations for the upgraded courses.
- Network with other data-science centres about computational teaching infrastructure.
- Sign up to help improve the R documentation page that courses link to.

Leads: Lykke Pedersen (UCPH), Sebastian Jakobsen (SDU). Advisory board: Curriculum
Transformation Board.
Basecamp: [WS:Curriculum upgrade](https://3.basecamp.com/6021648/projects/43870052)

### Research Project Consulting (`SV02`)

**Impact:** We bring data-science expertise into individual research projects, so
that researchers can do analyses, models and visualisations they could not do alone.

**Responsibilities**
- Act as the single point of entry for researchers who need data-science help on a project.
- Assess each request and match it to the right CPDSE expertise.
- Check capacity before accepting new projects.
- Keep an overview of ongoing projects and the people working on them.
- Make sure the work is done to a professional standard and handed over properly
  to the research group.
- Learn from finished projects to improve the service and identify recurring needs.

**Wanna help?** If you enjoy getting your hands dirty with real research data, this is
for you. And if your own project needs a data scientist, this is where to start. Get in touch
with Arnault.

Lead: Arnault-Quentin Vermillet (with Sarah Mittenentzwei at SDU later, if needed).
Advisory board: Institutional Advisory Board.

[Research Support Tasks & Data Science Projects](https://3.basecamp.com/6021648/projects/46868831).

### Research Group Strategy Consulting (`SV03`)

**Impact:** We help research groups and departments plan how to build their
data-science capability, so that they can strengthen their research in a lasting way
rather than project by project.

**Responsibilities**
- Engage research groups and departments that want to strengthen their data-science capability.
- Help them assess where they stand and agree on a strategy for where to go: skills,
  pipelines, infrastructure, hiring.
- Connect the strategy to the other services (People Development, Research Project
  Consulting) where that makes sense.
- Follow up with groups over time on whether the strategy is working.
- Feed recurring needs back to the Mission circle as input to CPDSE's priorities *(inferred)*.

**Wanna help?** Help research groups take the next step with data science, from
pipelines to hiring. Interested in joining a strategy conversation, or would your own group
like one? Get in touch with Morten.

Lead: Morten Lindow. Advisory board: Institutional Advisory Board.

## Fundamental (`CIR_FUNDAMENTAL`)

**Impact:** We maintain the shared structure CPDSE runs on: people, tools, spaces,
communication and ways of working, so that everyone in the centre can focus on their
work instead of the friction around it.

**Responsibilities**
- Own every operational responsibility that belongs neither to Services nor to the
  Research Community, so that nothing falls between circles.
- Make sure each responsibility has one named owner, and keep the register of owners current.
- Make it easy for members to see who owns what and to sign up as helpers.
- Keep shared systems, channels and spaces working and documented.
- Review responsibilities at each episode: add missing ones and retire those no longer needed.

**Wanna help?** Every area above has an owner who would love a helper, and most need
only a few hours. Pick something that sounds fun, sign up as a helper on the Basecamp to-do,
or talk to the owner. If something is missing, add it.

Staffed by the Nucleus and volunteers.
Basecamp: [Circle:Fundamental](https://3.basecamp.com/6021648/projects/48628160)

## Nucleus (`CIR_NUCLEUS`)

**Impact:** We connect and coordinate the work of Services, Fundamental and the Research
Community, so that priorities are aligned, nothing falls between chairs and the centre
moves as one.

**Responsibilities**
- Make sure every area and responsibility across the circles has a clear owner.
- Follow up on progress, and raise problems and dependencies early.
- Align priorities and resources across circles, within the direction set by the Mission circle.
- Run the weekly check-in that keeps the circles in step.
- Initiate and run sprints together with participants from across the centre.
- Pass information and decisions between the Mission circle and the rest of CPDSE.

**Wanna help?** Curious how the pieces fit together? Join a weekly check-in or a
sprint, or talk to Lykke.

Members: CPDSE's full-time staff and research group leaders. Lead: Lykke Pedersen
(as portfolio lead). Meets weekly.

## PharmaDataScience Research Community (`CIR_RESEARCH`)

*The spreadsheet calls this circle "Research"; WoW 2.0 renamed it the
"PharmaDataScience Research Community".*

**Impact:** We build a welcoming community of pharmaceutical data-science researchers
across groups and institutions, so that they can learn from each other, collaborate and
advance research they could not do alone.

**Responsibilities**
- Keep an overview of the research connected to CPDSE and of who knows what.
- Create regular ways for researchers to meet, share and exchange, such as Convergence
  Coffee and Talks, symposia, journal and method clubs, and writing retreats.
- Protect time for peer feedback on hard questions, pitches, manuscripts and grants.
- Support community experiments that members own, and continue those that create value.
- Run a fair and transparent process for allocating CPDSE research resources (for
  example PhD stipends and scholarships) in line with the centre's purpose.
- Welcome new researchers and students, and connect them to the CPDSE services where
  they can contribute.

**Wanna help?** Everybody doing pharmaceutical data-science research is welcome.
Drop by Convergence Coffee on Fridays, or join one of the community experiments from the
summer retreat:
- A writing retreat (Casper).
- Active exchange around shared scientific questions (Anders).
- Peer dialogues for PhD students (Finn).
- An infrastructure knowledge base, which still needs an owner.

Leads: Philipp Hans, Karlis Berzins, Jane Knöchel, Casper Steinmann. Main priority for
Season 4.
Basecamp: [Circle: Pharmaceutical Data Science Research Community](https://3.basecamp.com/6021648/projects/43870063)

## Mission (`CIR_MISSION`)

**Impact:** We set CPDSE's direction and secure the support of its foundations,
departments and board, so that the centre delivers lasting benefit to the pharma fields
during and after its funding period.

**Responsibilities**
- Develop CPDSE's positioning and strategy as its context changes, including its
  response to AI, and set each season's theme.
- Keep the finances in order: oversee the budget, prevent over- and underspending, and
  make high-level budget changes.
- Keep the foundations informed and engaged through reporting, dialogue meetings and KPIs.
- Keep the partner departments engaged, and make sure they benefit from CPDSE and
  support its strategy.
- Decide on hiring and on use of the agile hiring pool, delegating where appropriate.
- Delegate operational responsibilities and purchasing mandates in line with the principles.
- Bring the outside world into the community, with clear routes for external partners
  to take part.
- Make decisions visible to the whole organisation.
- Prepare in good time for what happens after the funding period.

Lead: Morten Lindow. Members: Jacob Kongsted, Lykke Pedersen, Jeppe Hartmann, SDU new hire.
